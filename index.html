    05

    Questions

    What is a price curve?

    It is a formula that sets a coin's price from how much of it has been bought. Early buyers pay less per coin, and each purchase moves the price up for the next one. Selling moves it back down.

    What happens when the curve fills up?

    On curves like the one modelled above, the coin reaches the end of its curve once the full amount set aside for sale has been bought. After that it can trade on open markets instead of the curve. Check how a specific launch handles this before you trade.

    Do you hold my funds?

    No. You trade from your own wallet and approve each transaction there. We never ask for a seed phrase or a private key, and anyone who does is not us.

    Is the curve above a real coin?

    No. It is a model with example numbers so you can feel how a curve behaves. The figures are not a quote and ignore fees.
